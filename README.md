## Research

**Trajectory prediction & evaluation**  
Applied contrastive learning in PyTorch to museum visitor trajectories, with evaluation on held-out visitors. Particular attention to cross-validation, data leakage and comparisons with simpler baselines.

**Spatial data & museum analytics**  
Structured visitor observations and museum layouts with Python and PostgreSQL/PostGIS: geometry reconstruction, adjacency validation and consistency checks.

Work carried out with **LS2N / DUKe** and the **Museology Lab at Ionian University**.

Co-author of two papers submitted to **EGC 2027 — under review**:
- *Visual Enrichment of BIRD for G-SITM*
- *Museum Trajectory Prediction: Heterogeneous Graphs and Contrastive Learning*
